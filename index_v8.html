Our Story

Six Decades. Three Generations. One Enduring Standard.

Established in 1962, MMH Sarees is the customer-facing identity of M/s M M Hosakoti, a third-generation cotton saree manufacturing firm based in Rabkavi Banhatti, Karnataka.

Our experience lies in understanding how yarn, weave, colour, border and pallu must work together. We manufacture 60's plain cotton, 60's jacquard cotton, cotton checks and rich pallu sarees with a focus on comfort, clarity and dependable quality.

We do not simply manufacture sarees—we help build lasting businesses through craftsmanship, consistency and trust. Our manufacturing solutions support wholesale supply, custom requirements and private-label partnerships across India.

How We Work

From Loom to Your Door

01
Yarn Procurement

Premium 60's count cotton yarn tested for tensile strength and colour consistency before entering production.

02
Warp Preparation

Warps are set with precision — pattern-specific threading ensures crisp check lines and clean jacquard structures.

03
Loom Weaving

Each construction is woven to its intended character, with careful attention to body, border, pallu and motif balance.

04
Quality Check

Every piece is checked for weave density, border finish, and colour uniformity before leaving the production floor.

05
Dispatch

Sarees are packed lot-wise with proper labelling and dispatched directly to your warehouse — on time, every time.

Frequently Asked Questions

Working with MMH Sarees

What sarees does MMH manufacture?

Our core range includes 60's plain cotton sarees, 60's jacquard cotton sarees, cotton checks and rich pallu cotton sarees.

Do you supply retailers and distributors across India?

Yes. MMH works with retailers, boutiques, distributors, online sellers and brands requiring dependable pan-India wholesale supply.

Does MMH offer private-label manufacturing?

Yes. Private-label and custom requirements are reviewed based on category, design direction, quantity, packaging and commercial feasibility.

Can packaging be customised?

Regular orders use standard MMH packaging. Packaging can be customised when a customer or business requirement calls for it.
