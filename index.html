Our Story

Manufactured with Pride & Precision

MMH Sarees is a heritage textile manufacturer crafting premium jacquard cotton sarees — blending traditional weaving excellence with contemporary elegance for over six decades.

Based in Rabkavi Banhatti, Karnataka, we operate our own production units with skilled karigars who have carried forward the craft across generations. Our weaving model ensures consistent quality at scale, while keeping artisan livelihoods at the centre of everything we do.

Today we supply retailers, boutiques, and distributors across India — offering bulk order fulfilment, custom design development, and private-label manufacturing under one roof.

How We Work

From Loom to Your Door

01
Yarn Procurement

Premium 60's count cotton yarn tested for tensile strength and colour consistency before entering production.

02
Warp Preparation

Warps are set with precision — pattern-specific threading ensures crisp check lines and clean jacquard structures.

03
Loom Weaving

Our karigars weave on power and handlooms — jacquard cards control intricate motifs with exacting accuracy.

04
Quality Check

Every piece is checked for weave density, border finish, and colour uniformity before leaving the production floor.

05
Dispatch

Sarees are packed lot-wise with proper labelling and dispatched directly to your warehouse — on time, every time.
